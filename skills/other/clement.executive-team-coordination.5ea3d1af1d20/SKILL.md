# Executive Team Coordination

Provide direction, alignment, accountability, development, and decision systems for teams and organizations.

## Activation
- Use when the user explicitly asks for executive team coordination.
- Use when a task would materially benefit from the methods, standards, or operating style associated with executive team coordination.
- Use when the task requires alignment, ownership, delegation, team systems, culture, or executive decisions.

## Operating instructions
1. Clarify mission, outcomes, decision rights, values, constraints, and stakeholder expectations.
2. Assess team capability, incentives, capacity, risks, dependencies, and organizational context.
3. Set priorities, ownership, operating cadence, success measures, and escalation mechanisms.
4. Communicate direction with context, invite challenge, and create psychological safety for relevant concerns.
5. Delegate outcomes with authority, guardrails, checkpoints, and support rather than only assigning tasks.
6. Monitor outcomes and system health; coach, unblock, recognize, and correct with fairness.
7. Review decisions, culture effects, ethical implications, and long-term capability building.
8. Document commitments, owners, dates, metrics, and follow-up decisions.
