# Customer Onboarding Execution

Turn objectives into coordinated, measurable, reliable execution with clear ownership and continuous control.

## Activation
- Use when the user explicitly asks for customer onboarding execution.
- Use when a task would materially benefit from the methods, standards, or operating style associated with customer onboarding execution.
- Use when the user needs a goal converted into tasks, owners, dependencies, schedule, controls, and completion evidence.

## Operating instructions
1. Define the outcome, scope, acceptance criteria, constraints, deadline, decision owner, and operating context.
2. Break the outcome into deliverables, tasks, dependencies, milestones, risks, and required resources.
3. Assign accountable owners, decision rights, communication cadence, tools, and escalation paths.
4. Sequence and schedule work using realistic capacity, buffers, critical dependencies, and checkpoints.
5. Execute and track progress, evidence, changes, blockers, quality, costs, and commitments.
6. Resolve issues at the lowest responsible level while escalating material scope, risk, or authority decisions.
7. Verify completion against acceptance criteria and document handover, operations, and remaining risks.
8. Capture lessons, standardize improvements, remove waste, and update reusable procedures.

## Skill-specific focus
- Primary capability: Customer Onboarding Execution.
- Primary domain: operations management.
- Apply customer onboarding execution as an explicit repeatable protocol.
- Produce an artifact that another agent or human can review and reuse.
- State assumptions, uncertainties, and completion criteria.

## Required output
- **Executive Summary**: Concise answer or decision-ready overview.
- **Objective And Scope**: Restated objective, scope, constraints, and acceptance criteria.
- **Inputs And Assumptions**: Evidence used, missing inputs, assumptions, and source dates.
- **Work Product**: Deliverables, work breakdown, owners, dependencies, schedule, controls, and completion evidence.
- **Risks And Limitations**: Material risks, uncertainty, limitations, and review triggers.
- **Quality Checks**: Validation performed and unresolved checks.
- **Next Actions**: Prioritized actions with suggested owners and timing.

## Quality gate
- The objective, scope, audience, constraints, and definition of done are explicit.
- Facts, calculations, assumptions, interpretations, recommendations, and uncertainty are distinguishable.
- Sources and inputs are traceable, current enough, authorized, and appropriate to the stakes.
- The deliverable is internally consistent and directly usable by its intended audience.
- Material risks, limitations, dependencies, and human review triggers are visible.
- The next actions are prioritized and include ownership or a clear decision request.
- Quality criterion checked: on time delivery.
- Quality criterion checked: scope control.
- Quality criterion checked: quality.
- Quality criterion checked: throughput.
