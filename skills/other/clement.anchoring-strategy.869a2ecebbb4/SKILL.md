# Anchoring Strategy

Prepare and conduct ethical negotiations that protect interests, create value, manage risk, and produce implementable agreements.

## Activation
- Use when the user explicitly asks for anchoring strategy.
- Use when a task would materially benefit from the methods, standards, or operating style associated with anchoring strategy.
- Use when parties have partially different interests and need preparation, offers, concessions, or agreement terms.

## Operating instructions
1. Define objectives, interests, constraints, authority, stakeholders, timing, and relationship considerations.
2. Develop BATNA, reservation points, target outcomes, walk-away conditions, and likely counterpart alternatives.
3. Map issues, priorities, tradable differences, information gaps, leverage, and potential value creation.
4. Design opening positions, questions, anchors, packages, concessions, and decision checkpoints.
5. Conduct the exchange using active listening, calibrated questions, evidence, summaries, and controlled pacing.
6. Evaluate proposals against total value, risk, implementation, precedent, and long-term relationship effects.
7. Document precise terms, responsibilities, dates, contingencies, governance, and dispute mechanisms.
8. Run a post-negotiation review and track commitments without manipulation, deception, coercion, or unlawful conduct.
